# Freelancer Client & Invoice Tracker — Lite

**Free Lite version.** Two databases (Clients + Invoices) that answer three questions: who owes you, how much, and what is overdue.

## What's in Lite

- **Clients** — name, email, currency, payment terms, notes.
- **Invoices** — invoice number, linked client, amount, currency, issue date, due date, status, paid date.
- **Overdue view** — sent invoices past their due date, worst first. Formula-driven: an invoice becomes overdue automatically the morning after its due date.
- **Unbilled work view** — draft invoices: work done, invoice not yet sent.
- **Per-client total outstanding** — a rollup on each client showing what they still owe.
- **Sample data** — 5 clients and 8 invoices (Paid / Sent / Overdue / Draft) so you can see every view working before adding your own data.

## How to Duplicate

1. Open the Duplicate link on the GitHub page and click **Duplicate** (top right). It copies the template into your own Notion workspace.
2. Replace the 5 sample clients with your own, then delete the 8 sample invoices.
3. Add your invoices. Status: `Draft` while writing, `Sent` when you send it, `Paid` when the money arrives — and fill in Paid date.

## How to use the two core views

- **Overdue** — check once a week. Every row here is money a client owes you past the agreed date. Follow up on the top row first (most days overdue). You never set "overdue" by hand; the formula does it.
- **Unbilled work** — check before the end of each month. Every row is work done but not yet invoiced. Send the invoice, then change its Status to `Sent`.
