# Freelancer Client & Invoice Tracker — Lite

**Free Lite version.** Two databases (Clients + Invoices) that answer three questions: who owes you, how much, and what is overdue.

## What's in Lite

- **Clients** — name, email, currency, payment terms, notes.
- **Invoices** — invoice number, linked client, amount, currency, issue date, due date, status, paid date.
- **Overdue view** — sent invoices past their due date, worst first. Driven by a formula, so an invoice becomes overdue automatically the morning after its due date. No manual updating.
- **Unbilled work view** — draft invoices: work done, invoice not yet sent.
- **Per-client total outstanding** — a rollup on each client showing what they still owe.
- **Sample data** — 5 clients, 8 invoices, covering Paid / Sent / Overdue / Draft, so you can see every view working before you add your own data.

## How to use it

1. Open the Duplicate link below and click **Duplicate** (top right). It copies the template into your own Notion workspace.
2. Replace the 5 sample clients with your own. Delete the 8 sample invoices.
3. Add your invoices. Set Status to `Draft` while writing, `Sent` when you send it, `Paid` when the money arrives (and fill in Paid date).
4. Check the **Overdue** view once a week. Check **Unbilled work** before the end of each month.

## What's not in Lite

- No Projects database. In Lite, unbilled work is tracked with invoice Status = `Draft`. The full version derives it from logged project work, which is more precise.
- No multi-currency conversion. Currency is recorded per client and per invoice; amounts are not converted.
- No tax or reporting views.
